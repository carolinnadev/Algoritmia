Tabla de Traza (N = 3)

+-----------+--------------------+-----------------------+------------------+
| Fila (i)  | Espacios (n - i)   | Asteriscos (2 * i - 1)| Linea generada   |
+-----------+--------------------+-----------------------+------------------+
|     1     | 3 - 1 = 2 espacios | 2(1) - 1 = 1 *        | "  *"            |
|     2     | 3 - 2 = 1 espacio  | 2(2) - 1 = 3 ***      | " ***"           |
|     3     | 3 - 3 = 0 espacios | 2(3) - 1 = 5 *****    | "*****"          |
+-----------+--------------------+-----------------------+------------------+
